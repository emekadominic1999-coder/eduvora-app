Study smarter for your university exams

Eduvora is a study app for Nigerian university students. Practise with CBT-style multiple-choice questions that explain every answer, see what your courses cover, find study materials, watch academic videos, work out your GPA and study with friends, all in one place.

It currently covers the University of Nigeria, Nsukka (UNN), with more universities coming.

Who it is for

Eduvora is built for undergraduates who want a calmer, more organised way to prepare for tests and exams. It is especially useful if you are in your first or second year and still learning how university assessment works, but it is open to any student at a school we cover.

What you can do with Eduvora

CBT exam practice

Take timed, computer-based style tests made of multiple-choice questions for your courses. After each paper you see your score and an explanation for every answer, so you learn from your mistakes instead of only counting them.

Course outlines

Browse the courses for your faculty, department and level, with course codes, titles and credit units. Know what you are expected to take before the semester starts. Learn how to read an outline.

Study materials

Find notes and handouts shared for your courses, and upload your own to help your classmates. Share only what you have the right to share.

GPA and CGPA calculator

Enter your courses, units and grades on the 5.0 scale and see your GPA and CGPA instantly. Need the method? Read our worked example.

Academic videos

Short video lessons that help you understand tough topics from a different angle than your lecture notes.

Study groups and community

Join study groups, ask questions, and talk with other students. A tutor marketplace is also there if you want one-to-one help.

How it works

  1. Create your account. Sign up with your email or Google and tell us your school, faculty, department and level.
  2. Pick your courses. Eduvora shows the outline, materials and practice papers that match your profile.
  3. Practise, review, improve. Take a CBT paper, read the explanations, and come back to the topics you missed.

Honest answers to common questions

Is Eduvora free?

You can start for free. Some CBT papers can be unlocked for a fee, and the price and what you get are always shown before you pay. See our Terms of Use for details.

Are the CBT questions real past exam papers?

No. They are practice questions written to help you revise. They are not official examination papers and they cannot predict what a lecturer will ask. Use them to test your understanding, not as a guarantee of what will appear.

Is Eduvora connected to my university?

No. Eduvora is an independent practice app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any university. School names and course codes are used only to show which school and course a page is for.

Which universities are covered?

We currently cover the University of Nigeria, Nsukka (UNN), and we plan to add more universities. Coverage differs by faculty and level, so some courses have more content than others.

Will using Eduvora guarantee better grades?

No app can promise that. Eduvora gives you practice, explanations and organisation tools. Results still depend on how consistently you study. Our study guides explain how to get the most from practice.

What happens to my data?

We explain what we collect and why in our Privacy Policy. We do not sell your personal data.

Who runs Eduvora?

It is an independent, student-focused project from Nigeria. Read more on the About page or contact us.
