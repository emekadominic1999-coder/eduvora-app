About Eduvora

Eduvora is an independent, student-focused project from Nigeria. It is not part of any university.

Who builds it

Eduvora is run by Dominic Emeka, as stated in our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. It is a small independent project, not a large company, and not an official product of any school. We build it with students in mind and improve it as we learn what students need.

Our mission

Many students prepare for exams with scattered notes, screenshots and guesswork. Our mission is to put the essentials in one place: a clear view of your courses, practice that explains itself, and simple tools for planning and tracking progress. We want studying to feel more organised and less stressful.

How content is created

  • Original explanations. Answer explanations and study guides are written for Eduvora. We do not copy them from other websites.
  • Practice questions, not official papers. CBT papers in Eduvora are practice questions written to help you revise. They are not official examination papers, and they are not past questions unless clearly stated.
  • Mistakes can happen. We work to keep questions accurate, but we cannot promise they are free of errors. Always check important facts with your lecturer, your course outline and recommended textbooks. If you spot a mistake, please tell us.
  • Student uploads. Some study materials are shared by students. People who upload must have the right to share what they post, and we may remove content that breaks our rules.

Where we cover

Eduvora currently covers the University of Nigeria, Nsukka (UNN), with more universities coming. Eduvora is an independent practice app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by UNN or any other university. School names and course codes are used only to show which school and course a page is for.

Our principles

  • Be honest about what Eduvora can and cannot do.
  • Protect student data and never sell it. See our Privacy Policy.
  • Explain answers, not just mark them.
  • Keep the core study tools accessible on a phone with a modest data plan.
