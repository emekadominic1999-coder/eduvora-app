Contact Eduvora

The best way to reach us is by email: hello@eduvora.ng.

What you can write to us about

  • Support: trouble signing in, or something in the app not working.
  • Payments: if you were charged but did not get access, include your payment reference.
  • Corrections: a question or explanation you think is wrong. Tell us the course and what you believe the right answer is.
  • Your data: requests to see, correct or delete your data, as described in our Privacy Policy.
  • Content concerns: if you believe something on Eduvora infringes your rights, see our Terms of Use and email us.
  • Feedback: ideas, requests for a course or school, and anything that would make Eduvora more useful.

Tips for a faster reply

  • Use the email address you signed up with, so we can find your account.
  • Tell us whether you use the website or the Android app.
  • Describe what happened and what you expected. A screenshot helps. Never send your password.

We read every message and reply as soon as we reasonably can.

Eduvora is an independent practice app. We cannot help with university admissions, registration, results or fees, because we are not connected to any university. Please contact your school directly for those.
